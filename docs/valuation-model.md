# Valuation Model: AI Engineering Platform

## Executive view

This is a strategic market-simulation valuation, not a claim of existing revenue or operating proof. It models what a premium AI engineering platform could plausibly be worth under a strategic acquisition or growth narrative.

## Current concept-stage valuation

### Conservative current value
- USD 2M–8M
- CAD 3M–11M

### Realistic current value
- USD 4M–12M
- CAD 6M–16M

### Premium scenario
- USD 8M–20M
- CAD 11M–28M

## Mature strategic product valuation

### Realistic high-upside product value
- USD 25M–60M
- CAD 35M–85M

### Premium strategic acquisition range
- USD 60M–120M+
- CAD 85M–170M+

## Value drivers

1. Category breadth
   - engineering platform, not a point feature

2. Developer workflow lock-in
   - high switching costs once integrated deeply

3. AI product adjacency
   - strong cross-over with developer productivity and enterprise software execution

4. Enterprise distribution
   - enterprise-oriented governance and security improve premium value

5. Strategic buyer fit
   - direct adjacency for dev-tool, cloud, and AI platform acquirers

6. Recurring revenue potential
   - SaaS + enterprise + governance + self-hosted + support services

## Quality of evidence required to defend premium valuation

To seriously justify a $25M–$120M+ valuation, a product would need:

- live customer adoption
- enterprise pilot results
- measurable work acceleration or code quality improvements
- security and governance maturity
- strong developer product-market fit
- documentation and support maturity
- product-led or sales-led distribution traction

## Scenario model

### Scenario A: strong early traction
- 5,000 paying users
- $45/user/month average
- ARR: $2.7M
- enterprise add-ons
- strategic value: $15M–$40M

### Scenario B: premium product narrative
- 20,000 users
- $60/user/month average
- ARR: $14.4M
- enterprise contracts and self-hosted product
- strategic value: $40M–$120M+

### Scenario C: platform-scale strategic asset
- 50,000+ seats or major enterprise contracts
- multi-product engineering platform narrative
- strategic value: $80M–$200M+

## Buy-side interpretation

A strategic buyer is more likely to pay a premium if the product is positioned as:

- a software delivery operating system
- a developer intelligence layer
- an AI-native engineering platform
- a strategic workflow engine for software organizations

Not as a narrow coding assistant.

## Final conclusion

The right market simulation is not “GitHub clone value.” It is a premium developer platform valuation modeled around AI-native workflow orchestration, enterprise trust, and strategic product lock-in.

This supports a realistic premium market narrative in the tens of millions for a successful execution scenario.
