# Valuation Model in CAD

## Executive framing

This valuation model is a strategic scenario, not a representation of actual operating revenue or proven customer adoption.

It models the value of an AI Engineering Platform in a premium strategic context.

## Current concept-stage valuation

### Realistic starting value
- CAD 180,000 to CAD 420,000
- Mid-case estimate: CAD 280,000

### Why this value is credible

- strong technical concept and architecture
- clear category framing and product story
- real strategic relevance to developer tooling and AI workflows
- not yet proven in the market, so it is not a full SaaS valuation

## Mature product valuation

### Target strategic value
- CAD 8,000,000 to CAD 25,000,000

### Premium strategic range
- CAD 15,000,000 to CAD 45,000,000

### Extreme case
- CAD 45,000,000 to CAD 70,000,000

## Value drivers

1. Category breadth
2. AI workflow lock-in
3. enterprise governance and compliance value
4. developer productivity leverage
5. multi-product platform potential
6. strategic buyer adjacency

## Likely model for premium valuation

A buyer pays when the platform is positioned as:

- a software delivery operating system
- a trusted AI-native engineering platform
- a platform with durable workflow integration
- not a narrow coding helper

## Sales and revenue concept

### Example pricing
- developer: CAD 45 to CAD 90 per month
- team: CAD 180 to CAD 350 per user per month
- enterprise: custom contracts
- self-hosted: custom annual enterprise agreement

### Revenue simulation
- 1,000 users at CAD 60 monthly = CAD 720,000 ARR
- 10,000 users at CAD 70 monthly = CAD 8.4M ARR
- enterprise add-ons can significantly exceed this

## Recommendation

Use these valuation bands:

- current concept: CAD 180,000–420,000
- mature strategic product: CAD 8,000,000–25,000,000
- premium strategic outcome: CAD 15,000,000–45,000,000

This is the most defensible CAD-based strategic simulation.
